# Recorded audio upgrade

Seventeen locally hosted CC0 audio excerpts replace the primary synthesized soundtrack and impacts. See public/audio-manifest.js for individual source pages, attribution, conversions, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. In-game credits are at /audio-credits.html. No runtime third-party audio requests.

Music, SFX and Ambience sliders persist locally. Solo: pause menu. Arcade: Audio mix. A Play/start gesture unlocks audio. Three persistent loop sources crossfade between exploration and combat; pausing suspends playback rather than restarting music. Hidden tabs and mute stop transient effects. Confirmed combat events drive impacts, with pitch variation, a bass layer and temporary music ducking. Nearby solo enemy windups trigger warnings. The existing installed-voice announcer remains; it is not a recorded voice actor.

Movement, damage, controls, saved profiles and backend contracts are unchanged. Missing audio cannot prevent play. Arcade retains synthesized impact fallback while downloads are unavailable. Audio is normalized, but listening balance on real phone speakers/headphones remains a manual acceptance check. These recordings are licensed assets, not a claim of an original commissioned score.
